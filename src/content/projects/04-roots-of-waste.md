I designed a website to showcase three original pieces: a tree, a seal, and a California poppy. Each illustration was built in Photoshop by individually placing and layering pieces of trash to form symbolic figures that represent the natural environment, local wildlife, and the specific environmental challenges facing the Bay.

Every detail in the three graphics is made solely out of trash. With 57 million tons of plastic pollution accumulating every year, the message is simple: let us not be rooted from waste.

This project let me explore the impact of waste through research, and showed me how visuals with a purpose can tell a deeper story.
