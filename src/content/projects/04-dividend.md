Our goal as designers was not just to make things look good, but to create work that elevated each artist's brand. We did this by drafting and exploring different styles to capture the unique vibe and message of each artist.

Some of the design projects included logos, wallpapers, presentations, and tour posters. My design of the Marisa Sardo Tour poster was featured on both Dividend's and the artist's Instagram accounts.

Through this internship, I learned to adapt creatively and design with intention.
